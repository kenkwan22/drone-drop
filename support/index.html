Support

Drone Drop, by Mont Saba Works.

Something not working, or a question about the game? We are happy to help.

Contact us or report a problem (opens our support tracker on GitHub; a free GitHub account is needed to post).

My purchase did not unlock the levels

Open the paywall (finish level 8, or tap a locked level), then tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with. If that does not work, contact us and tell us your device and the date of purchase.

I bought it on one phone and want it on another

Install the game, open the paywall and tap Restore purchase using the same Apple ID or Google account.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play (support.google.com/googleplay). When a refund is approved the extra levels lock again.

My progress disappeared

Progress is saved on the device, so deleting the app or clearing a browser's site data erases it. Purchases are not affected; use Restore purchase.

How do I play?

Drag your finger left and right (or use the arrows or keyboard keys) to slide the vehicle under the falling parcels. Tap the water-drop button to spray the pelican, seagulls and the ape. Catch five in a row for turbo. The How to play button on the title screen has the full guide.
