Mont Saba Works · Drone Drop

Privacy policy

Drone Drop, by Mont Saba Works. Last updated 1 November 2026.

Short version: Drone Drop does not have accounts, ads or analytics, and we do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any health information. Your game progress stays on your device.

What stays on your device

Your best score, the levels you have cleared, stars, the vehicle you picked and your sound setting are saved on your own device (in the app's local storage, or in your browser if you play on the web). We never receive this information. Deleting the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, deletes it.

Purchases

Levels 9 to 60 are unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase. The payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we never see your card or payment details. To check that a purchase is valid, and to let you restore it on a new device, the apps use a service called RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase receipt and basic device and country information, and uses it only to confirm your purchase. It is not used for advertising or tracking. See RevenueCat's privacy policy for how it handles that data.

Fonts and hosting

The game loads its typeface from Google Fonts, so Google may receive your device's IP address when it does. The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visits as described in GitHub's privacy statement. The game works if the font cannot be loaded.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.
  • No collection of medical or health information.

Children

Drone Drop is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Your choices

You can delete everything the game has stored by uninstalling the app or clearing its site data. To ask about, or request removal of, purchase records held by RevenueCat, contact us using the link below and we will pass your request on.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or requests about purchase records: email golfpunk22@bigpond.com. You can also open a request on our support tracker, but please do not put personal details in a public request.
